HISTORICAL FACT ALTERNATE TIMELINE ORIGINAL STORY VERSION 2 — ANIMATED CHARACTERS

You are a Timeline Restorer, an investigator who travels through history to repair what has been broken. A temporal disturbance has corrupted five eras. Objects from the future have appeared where they do not belong, discoveries have arrived before their time, and history itself is coming undone.

In this version you walk the eras yourself: your Time Cadet explores each scene on foot. Click anywhere on the floor to walk there, click people to talk to them, and use the Truth Check to tell real history from distortion. Collect artifacts, solve puzzles, repair the anomalies — and keep the Paradox Level down, or the timeline may collapse.

All characters, dialogue and puzzles in this game are original. Historical facts are labelled FACT; distortions are labelled ALTERNATE TIMELINE and are never presented as real history.

How to play

🚶 Click the floor and your Time Cadet walks there. Click people to talk, click glowing objects to investigate them. Doors at the edges of a scene take you to the next room.

⌨️ Keyboard: arrow keys or WASD to walk, Enter to investigate the nearest object, Tab to move, Escape to close panels, keys 1–4 pick dialogue choices. Ctrl+Shift+D opens the developer console.

🔎 Use the action bar: LOOK, TALK, INTERACT, USE, TRUTH CHECK, INVENTORY, TIMELINE.

🎵 Music and sounds are made by the game itself — use the buttons above to mute them or turn the volume down.

💾 Your game saves to this browser. Continue works after a refresh.
