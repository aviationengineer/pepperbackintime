HISTORICAL FACT ALTERNATE TIMELINE ORIGINAL STORY

You are a Timeline Restorer, an investigator who travels through history to repair what has been broken. A temporal disturbance has corrupted five eras. Objects from the future have appeared where they do not belong, discoveries have arrived before their time, and history itself is coming undone.

Investigate each scene. Use the Truth Check to tell real history from distortion. Collect artifacts, solve puzzles, repair the anomalies — and keep the Paradox Level down, or the timeline may collapse.

All characters, dialogue and puzzles in this game are original. Historical facts are labelled FACT; distortions are labelled ALTERNATE TIMELINE and are never presented as real history.

How to play

🖱️ Click glowing objects and people to select them, then use the action bar: LOOK, TALK, INTERACT, USE, TRUTH CHECK, INVENTORY, TIMELINE.

⌨️ Keyboard: Tab to move, Enter to choose, Escape to close panels, keys 1–4 pick dialogue choices. Ctrl+Shift+D opens the developer console.
